I am currently in the third year of my PhD investigating multisensory information processing and social behaviours in intertidal scavengers. My interests include sensory and behavioural biology and ecology, as well as science communication and the intersections of science and society. I enjoy writing about animal behaviours, how humans impact them, and how science is used and portrayed in literature and the arts.
