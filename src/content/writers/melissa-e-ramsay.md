I am currently in the third year of my PhD investigating multisensory information processing and social behaviours in intertidal scavengers. My interests include sensory and behavioural biology and ecology, as well as science communication and the intersections of science and society. I am excited to be an editor for Parallax to help support developing writers and make all forms of science accessible to our readers!
